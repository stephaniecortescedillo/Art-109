About Me ≽◉˕ ◉≼

Hello everyone! I am Stephanie Cortes, a college student currently pursuing a BFA in Digital Media Art. I enjoy embracing new technology and experimenting with software to translate my ideas into a "physical form." My artwork is usually composed of mixed media elements, such as digital illustration, photography, 3d modeling, and animation. I have experience with software like Blender and Adobe programs such as Photoshop and Illustrator. Through my practice, I aim not only to explore my identity and creative expression through digital experimentation but also to create visually engaging artwork that can help bring color and happiness into the world.
